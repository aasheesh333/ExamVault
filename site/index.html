ExamVault policies

ExamVault is a free study utility for Indian competitive-exam aspirants. It needs no account and keeps your admit cards, roll numbers and portal details encrypted on your own device.

  • Privacy policy — what is stored, what leaves your device, and what never does.
  • Terms of use — the terms that cover your use of the app.

The app itself is open source at github.com/aasheesh333/ExamVault.

ExamVault is a free study utility for Indian competitive-exam aspirants. It is not affiliated with any examining body. Questions: open an issue.
